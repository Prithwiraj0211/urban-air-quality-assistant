# 🌫️ AeroShield AI — Urban Air Quality & Winter Smog Assistant
> **PromptWars [Challenge 6]: Urban Air Quality Assistant**

AeroShield AI is a GenAI-powered household and community assistant designed to help urban families navigate severe winter smog episodes. It translates real-time air quality metrics and household context into personalized, low-emission daily routines, DIY indoor air quality (IAQ) hacks, and sustainable lifestyle swaps—while quantifying the **direct health and environmental impact** of every action.

---

## 1. Chosen Vertical & Target Persona
* **Challenge Vertical:** `[Challenge 6] Urban Air Quality Assistant`
* **Primary Persona:** Urban families (including children, elderly members, and daily commuters) living in high-AQI metropolitan regions prone to winter thermal inversions (e.g., Delhi-NCR, Kolkata, Lahore, Beijing).
* **Core Pain Point:** Generic AQI apps only display hazardous numbers without telling families *when* to ventilate, *how* to protect vulnerable members indoors affordably, or *how much* their daily commuting and heating choices reduce personal PM2.5 exposure and community emissions.

---

## 2. Approach & Logical Decision-Making
The assistant uses a **hybrid deterministic + Generative AI architecture** to ensure reliability, safety, and zero-friction evaluation:

1. **Environmental Context Ingestion:** Fetches live pollutant data (`PM2.5`, `PM10`, `NO2`, `US/EU AQI`) via the Open-Meteo Air Quality API, alongside an interactive **Winter Smog Simulator** slider (AQI `50` to `500+`) for testing extreme inversion scenarios.
2. **Household Vulnerability Profiling:** Weighs risk multipliers based on user inputs:
   * *Vulnerable Groups:* Infants/children, elderly, respiratory/asthma conditions.
   * *Lifestyle & Exposure Vectors:* Commute mode (two-wheeler, car, public transit, WFH), cooking fuel, and home ventilation setup.
3. **Structured GenAI Reasoning (`gemini-2.5-flash`):** Synthesizes environmental severity + household profile into strict JSON schemas covering three actionable pillars:
   * **Time-Blocked Daily Routine** (aligned with winter thermal inversion lift windows).
   * **Low-Cost Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) Hacks** (e.g., DIY Corsi-Rosenthal filtration, wet-mopping, strategic cross-ventilation).
   * **Sustainable Alternatives** (e.g., transit shifts, induction cooking, staggered errands).
4. **Offline Smart Fallback Engine:** If no Gemini API key is supplied, a deterministic rule-based engine dynamically generates tailored recommendations so the app works immediately out-of-the-box.

---

### Key Features
* **Direct Impact Explainer:** Every recommendation displays quantified health/emission benefits (e.g., *"-35% indoor PM2.5 exposure"* or *"-2.1 kg CO2e/day"*).
* **Interactive Impact Simulator:** Checking off recommended habits dynamically recalculates the household's **Exposure Reduction Score** and **Community Emission Savings**.
* **WCAG AA Accessibility:** Color-blind-safe AQI severity badges (combining icons, text labels, and high-contrast palettes) with full keyboard navigation.

---

## 4. Assumptions Made
1. **Winter Thermal Inversion Pattern:** Outdoor PM2.5 concentrations peak between `06:00–10:00` and `19:00–23:00` due to boundary-layer compression, with a safer dispersion window between `13:00–16:00` for brief natural ventilation.
2. **Impact Estimation Baselines:** Percentage reductions in indoor PM2.5 and CO2e savings are modeled using peer-reviewed environmental engineering averages (e.g., HEPA/Corsi-Rosenthal filtration reducing indoor particulates by ~45–60%; shifting a 15 km solo petrol car commute to metro/public transit saving ~2.4 kg CO2e).
3. **Zero-Secret Security:** API keys are never stored in the repository; users or evaluators can optionally input a Gemini API key in the UI session or run in instant Demo Mode.

